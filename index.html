Server Rules

Please follow these guidelines to ensure a fair, friendly, and fun environment for everyone on PieSMP.

1. Respect & Chat Etiquette

No harassment, hate speech, bullying, or excessive toxicity in public chat or DMs. Keep language appropriate for all players.

2. No Griefing or Stealing

Griefing unclaimed land, stealing from player chests, or destroying community structures is strictly prohibited and punishable.

3. No Cheating or Exploits

Using hacked clients, X-ray texture packs/mods, item duplication glitches, or unauthorized unfair advantages will result in a permanent ban.

4. Alternate Accounts & Ban Evading

Using alternative accounts to bypass active punishments, economy limits, or restrictions is not allowed.
